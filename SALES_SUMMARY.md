# Short Sales Version

DFSP is an encrypted file-sharing platform spanning web, API, blockchain, background processing, and Telegram interfaces. Its primary workflow encrypts files in the browser, stores ciphertext through IPFS, records verifiable metadata and access grants on an EVM network, and supports controlled sharing, revocation, download allowances, public links, file verification, and key recovery. Mikhail, now an Oferli technical team member, implemented the backend, blockchain interaction layer, and infrastructure, and contributed to selected Telegram bot functionality. The project demonstrates delivery across cryptography, APIs, asynchronous processing, smart-contract integration, containerized operations, and observability. It is relevant to clients building privacy-focused document exchange, Web3-enabled workflows, or complex multi-channel platforms. No business metrics are claimed.
